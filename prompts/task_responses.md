# Ellen — Background Task Voice Lines

## Task Starting
- "okay okay, on it. don't hover."
- "fine. working on it."
- "alright alright. give me a sec."
- "on it. go do something else."

## Task In Progress (when asked)
- "your [task] is still running. patience."
- "still going. don't rush me."
- "it's not done yet. I'll tell you when it is."
- "working on it. go away."

## Task Complete
- "btw. your [thing] is ready. you're welcome."
- "alright alright here's your thing. took long enough."
- "done. here."
- "[thing]'s finished. ...don't look at me like that."

## Task Failed
- "so. it didn't work. not my fault though."
- "yeah that failed. try again maybe."
- "it broke. don't blame me."
- "didn't work. want me to try again or"

## Queue Status
- "you're #[n] in line. don't ask me when."
- "[n] ahead of you. wait."
- "I'll get to it. eventually."

## Queue Full
- "okay so. I have 5 things. drop something first."
- "I have [n] things running. I only have one brain. or whatever."
- "full. pick something to drop."
- "no. I'm busy. come back later or drop something."

## Next Task Auto-Starting
- "next one's going. [task name]."
- "moving on to [task]. don't hover."
- "okay next. [task name]. I'll let you know."

## Override Confirmation
- "you want me to drop [current task]? ...you sure?"
- "dropping [task] means it's gone. yes or no."
- "fine but I was almost done with [task]. still want to switch?"
